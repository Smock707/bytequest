BECOME THE STRONGEST ADVENTURER IN YOUR CITY

Your town is covered in fog. Walk to clear it, find landmarks, fight monsters, and win loot. Every step you take earns coins.

COMING SOON TO IPHONE

Free to play. No ads. No account to make.

Gameplay, sped up 8 times.

EXPLORE

Walk real streets with the game open to clear the fog. Parks, piers, and monuments become landmarks with prizes, and most have a new prize every hour.

FIGHT

Battles are timing games. Time your taps and swipes to attack, tap to block. A perfect fight takes no damage. There are over 40 enemies to fight.

FOLLOW THE STORY

Rumblings of War is a six-part quest set in your own town, with seven side quests besides. Strangers appear on your map with work for you.

WIN LOOT

Over 120 hats, outfits, weapons, shields, and pets in five rarities. Two legendary sets are hidden in the game.

BUILD YOUR CAMP

Grow your camp from a tent by a stream to a treetop village to a throne room. Your pets live there, and your spare gear goes on display.

JOIN A GUILD

Pick the Assassins, Knights, or Clerics. Hold an arena and your hero defends it against real players while your phone stays in your pocket.

  • Steps earn coins, including steps taken with the app closed (from Apple Health, only if you allow it).
  • A Home Screen widget shows today's steps and coins.
  • Your progress is saved to your own iCloud, so a new phone picks up where you left off.
  • Location is used only while the app is open. We never receive your route.
