Report a player name

Use "Report this name" on any arena screen — reported names are masked on your device instantly and reviewed on our side, typically within 24 hours. "Block this player" hides a champion on your device permanently. You can also report names by email.

Common questions

Steps aren't earning coins. Coins for closed-app walks come from Apple Health: check Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → ByteQuest and allow reading Steps. In-app walking needs Location "While Using the App".

The map isn't revealing. Make sure Location access is "While Using the App" with Precise Location on. Fog only burns away while the app is open — that's by design (and by battery).

Arenas say the ledger can't be reached. Coliseums need an iCloud account signed in on the device and a network connection. Everything else in the game works without them.

Cancel or manage ByteQuest Plus. Subscriptions are handled by Apple: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or the "Manage Subscription" link on the Plus card in the Shop. Cancelling keeps Plus active until the end of the paid period.

Purchases missing after a reinstall. Open the Shop and tap "Restore Purchases" — your subscription and gem balance re-sync through your Apple Account.

Signed in with Apple on a new phone? Sign-in personalizes your hero locally; game saves live on the device, so a new phone starts a fresh map. Email us if something looks wrong.

Lost progress? ByteQuest keeps a local backup save and restores it automatically if the main save is damaged. If something still looks wrong, email us before deleting the app — deleting the app erases your map for good.
